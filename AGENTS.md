## Screen use

Run automated development and verification headlessly. The user needs their
screen. Do not open visible application windows or browser tabs, take over
fullscreen, or capture their desktop unless they explicitly request that action.
Use Qt's offscreen platform and exported images/video for visual checks.

The product's audience output is the visual explanation itself. Do not treat a
webpage, operator dashboard, slide cards or presentation chrome as that output.
Research tools and any operator controls belong backstage. Dynamic motion should
express the explanation and stay grounded in the source material.
